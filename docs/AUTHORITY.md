# Authority and provenance

Naming confidence should be explicit:

- `EXACT`: name/meaning directly proven by surviving literal/API/resource identity.
- `CROSS_BUILD`: transferred from a previously proven logical class/member.
- `RESEARCH`: proven by SpawnPK-Src exact-client archaeology.
- `INFERRED_HIGH`: multiple independent static signals agree.
- `INFERRED_LOW`: plausible convenience name; not authoritative.
- `UNKNOWN`: intentionally left mechanical.

Every semantic rename should retain its evidence and confidence instead of silently
turning inference into fact.
